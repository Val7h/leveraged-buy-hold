# QUICK REFERENCE: CHANNEL CAC MATH

**Organic (Content):**
- Cost: $0–200 per signup
- Timeline: 3 months to see results
- Breakeven: Month 6–8

**Direct Sales (Advisors):**
- Cost: $0 per signup (founder-led)
- Timeline: 4–6 weeks per advisor
- Payoff: 20–30 users per advisor = huge ROI

**Community (Discord/Referral):**
- Cost: $50–100 per signup
- Timeline: Immediate (2 weeks)
- Payoff: Viral coefficient adds 20%+ growth

**Goal:** Blend all 3 to hit <$50 blended CAC

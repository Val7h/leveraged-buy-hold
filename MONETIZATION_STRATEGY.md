## 🎁 Feature Positioning

### Asset Comparison Table ($99 feature lock)

**Problem:** Comparing 5 assets takes 5 minutes per asset (25 min)  
**Solution:** LBH shows all 5 in 1 minute  
**Value:** "Save 24 minutes per screening session"

**Messaging:**
```
"Instead of checking one asset at a time,
compare all candidates side-by-side.

See quality scores, entry signals, and metrics
at a glance. Make better decisions faster."
```

**Use Case:**
- Screened 20 assets? Compare top 5 in 60 seconds
- Benchmark against each other
- Find "Best Now" vs "Best Quality"

---

### Backtest Comparison Panel ($299 feature lock)

**Problem:** Backtesting takes effort, but knowing if it's good is unclear  
**Solution:** LBH automatically compares vs. B&H and tells you if strategy is viable

**Value:** "Confidence to deploy live trading"

**Messaging:**
```
"Backtesting shows past performance.
Backtest Comparison shows if it's BETTER than alternatives.

Is your 2.5x Adaptive strategy better than B&H 2x?
LBH answers in 4 metrics: CAGR, Sharpe, Drawdown, Return.

Deploy with confidence or optimize before trading."
```

**Use Case:**
- Created new strategy? Verify it's actually better
- Comparing leverage levels? See which wins
- Risk nervous? Show yourself the math works

---

### Portfolio Sector Breakdown ($299 feature lock)

**Problem:** Investors accidentally concentrate into one sector  
**Solution:** Visual breakdown prevents concentration risk

**Value:** "Catch diversification mistakes before they hurt"

**Messaging:**
```
"You think you're diversified. Really?

See exactly what % of your money is in each sector.
40% Utilities? 30% Healthcare? Good.
70% Technology? Red flag.

Rebalance before market shifts punish you."
```

**Use Case:**
- Spot concentration risk (>60% in 1 sector)
- Prevent sector-specific downturns from destroying portfolio
- Macro-aware rebalancing

## 💡 Messaging Tips

### What NOT to say
❌ "Compare multiple assets"  
✅ "Spend 60 seconds, not 25 minutes, choosing your next investment"

❌ "Backtest comparison shows alternatives"  
✅ "Finally know if your strategy actually beats buy-and-hold"

❌ "Portfolio diversification monitoring"  
✅ "Stop accidentally concentrating all your money in one sector"

### The Power of Specificity
```
❌ Weak: "Advanced analytics"
✅ Strong: "Kelly Criterion automatically sizes positions"

❌ Weak: "Saves time"
✅ Strong: "From 25 minutes to 60 seconds per screening"

❌ Weak: "Better risk management"
✅ Strong: "Catch concentration above 60% before it destroys your portfolio"
```

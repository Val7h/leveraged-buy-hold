## IDEAL CUSTOMER PROFILE (1 PAGE)

### Primary Persona: "Bruno, Investidor Defensivo"

**Demographics:**
- Age: 35–55 years old
- Location: Brazil (primary), USA/Europe (secondary)
- Income: $100k–500k USD/year
- Invested Assets: $500k–$5M

**Psychographics:**
- Buy & Hold philosophy (not day-trading)
- Seeks passive/semi-passive strategy
- Risk-averse but wants better returns than CDI (Cert. of Deposit)
- Values time (doesn't want to monitor daily)
- Tech-savvy enough for app (uses ETFs, understands indices)

**Goals:**
- Generate 10–15% annual returns (CAGR)
- Sleep soundly (doesn't panic on volatility)
- Use leverage **safely** (with guardrails, not recklessly)
- Automate decision-making (reduce manual rebalancing)

**Pain Points:**
1. **Manual portfolio management is slow:** Spreadsheets, email alerts, manual rebalancing
2. **Leverage is confusing & dangerous:** Margin calls, liquidation risk, no smart sizing
3. **No monitoring:** Market moves, but I'm busy; miss opportunities
4. **FOMO on better returns:** CDI at 9% doesn't feel enough; S&P 500 is risky without leverage
5. **Advisor/broker costs:** Traditional advisors charge 0.5–2% AUM; brokers charge commissions

**Buying Triggers:**
- Achieved first $500k invested
- Recently inherited money / got bonus
- Looking to optimize portfolio returns
- Found about LBH via blog post or friend referral
- Frustrated with spreadsheet-based tracking

**Decision Criteria:**
1. Compliance & regulatory safety (CVM approval in Brazil)
2. Simplicity (not want to build own model)
3. Proven backtest results (20+ years)
4. Affordable pricing (<$100/month)
5. Mobile access (check portfolio on phone)

## UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION

### One-liner:
> **"The smart way to invest with leverage—automated, safe, defensively focused."**

### Core Pillars:

**1. Intelligent Leverage (Not Manual)**
- RSI + Score-based (not guessing)
- Increases on dips (buy low), decreases on rallies (sell high)
- Natural de-leveraging via dividends
- Kelly Criterion + VaR guardrails

**2. Proven Backtest + Simulator**
- 20 years of historical data (S&P 500, individual stocks)
- Monte Carlo: Probability of ruin, percentile outcomes
- Stress tested on 3 major crises (2008, 2020, 2022)
- Transparent methodology (not black box)

**3. Simplified Dashboard + Alerts**
- Real-time scoring of portfolio opportunities
- Automatic alerts (RSI oversold, score spikes)
- Mobile-first design (check anytime)
- Export & share results

**4. Defensive Focus**
- Scoring weights: 60% quality (beta, dividend, sharpe), 40% opportunity
- Prioritizes stable sectors: Utilities, Healthcare, Consumer Staples
- Doesn't promote HFT or risky speculation
- Built for 10+ year horizon

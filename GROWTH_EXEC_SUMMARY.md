## REVENUE MODEL & UNIT ECONOMICS

### Pricing (Approved by Finance, Day 7):
- **Free Tier:** 5 assets, no backtest → 80–85% of signups (conversion funnel)
- **Pro Tier:** $29/month → Backtest, simulator, 10 assets, email alerts → 15–18% convert
- **Premium Tier:** $99/month → Unlimited, API, SMS alerts → 2–3% convert
- **Enterprise:** Custom (advisors, brokers) → $500–2k/month

### Blended ARPU:
- 70% of paid users @ $29 = $20.30
- 28% of paid users @ $99 = $27.72
- 2% of paid users @ $500+ = $10.00
- **Blended: $58/month → but conservative estimate $45/month**

### Unit Economics:
- **CAC:** $50 (blended across channels)
- **Payback Period:** ($45 × 85% margin) / $50 = **1.1 months** ✅
- **LTV (36-month horizon, 5% monthly churn):** $1,230 lifetime value
- **LTV:CAC:** 24.6:1 ✅ (target >3:1)
- **Gross Margin:** 85% (SaaS margin)

**Sensitivity:**
- If CAC rises to $100 → payback = 2.2 months (still healthy)
- If churn rises to 10% → LTV drops to $720 (still viable, but tighter)

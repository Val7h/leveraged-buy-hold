## 2. UNIQUE VALUE PROPOSITION

> **"Automated leverage sizing for defensive buy-and-hold investors—no spreadsheets, no stress, proven over 20 years."**

### Core Pillars

**1. Smart Leverage (Not Manual)**
- RSI + Score-based automatic position sizing
- Increases on dips, decreases on rallies
- Natural de-leveraging via dividends
- Kelly Criterion guardrails prevent margin calls

**2. Proven Backtest + Simulator**
- 20 years of historical data
- Monte Carlo: probability of ruin, percentile outcomes
- Stress tested on 3 major crises (2008, 2020, 2022)
- Transparent methodology (not black box)

**3. Simplified Dashboard + Alerts**
- Real-time scoring of portfolio opportunities
- Automatic alerts (RSI oversold, score spikes)
- Mobile-first design
- Export & share results

### Positioning vs Competitors

| Feature | LBH | IB | Quantfury | DIY |

| Leverage Automation | ✅ Smart | ❌ Manual | ✅ Generic | ❌ No |

## FAQ SECTION

### Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Can I try Pro free?
**A:** Yes! All new users get a 14-day free trial of Pro (no credit card required for the first 7 days; we ask for a card on Day 8 to activate the trial, but you won't be charged until Day 15).

**More info:**
- 14 days of unlimited access to all Pro features
- No charges before Day 15
- If you cancel before Day 15, you're never charged
- After Day 15: Auto-renews at $19/month
- Cancel anytime; refund available in first 7 days

---

#### Q: Do prices ever change?
**A:** No. All users who sign up in 2026 get **grandfathered pricing** — your price is locked for 2 years minimum, even if we raise prices later.

**Early adopter benefit:**
- Join June-July 2026: Price locked until June-July 2028
- Price increase expected Q1 2027 (new users only)
- Existing customers keep original price (unless they choose to upgrade)

---

#### Q: What if I don't like Pro?
**A:** No problem. You have **7 days for a full refund** after your first charge (Day 15).

**Pro tier refund policy:**
- Charge Day 15: Get a 7-day money-back guarantee
- Cancel by Day 22: Full refund, no questions
- Cancel after Day 22: Month-to-month billing; cancel anytime
- Pro-rated refunds: If you cancel mid-month, you get a refund for unused days

**For example:**
- You charge Day 15, cancel Day 19: Get full $19 refund
- You charge Day 15, cancel Day 30: No refund, but your next billing is July 15 (month-to-month)

#### Q: Why $19/month and not $9 or $29?
**A:** Great question. Here's the reasoning:

**$9/month:**
- Too cheap for value (implies not serious)
- Forces volume: need 2,500+ Pro users for profitability
- Attracts bargain hunters, not committed investors
- Low lifetime value (more churn)

**$19/month (chosen):**
- Signals "serious professional tool" without breaking bank
- Affordable for solo investors ($228/year = coffee budget)
- Works for both retail ($50k-$500k AUM) and advisors
- Supports team payroll + infrastructure (sustainable)
- International acceptable (similar to other SaaS)

**$29/month:**
- Might work, but adds friction for free → pro conversion
- 20-30% conversion drop expected vs $19
- Test later if $19 saturates

**Validation:**
- Comparable tools: Quantfury ($20), Portfolio Labs ($15-25)
- Our value: automation + deep backtest = worth $19

### Tier Upgrade/Downgrade

#### Q: Can I upgrade mid-month?
**A:** Yes, instantly.

**How it works:**
- Upgrade Free → Pro: Immediate (charges pro-rated amount for remaining days)
- Example: Upgrade on June 20: Charge $12.13 (12 days remaining in month) 
- Next charge: July 20 (full $19)

**Downgrade Pro → Free:**
- Immediate
- Pro features disabled right away
- No refund (you paid for the month)
- Portfolios/data preserved

---

#### Q: What's the pro-rated charge?
**A:** Calculated daily.

**Formula:**
```
Daily rate = $19 / 30 days = $0.63/day
Remaining days in month × $0.63 = Pro-rated charge
```

**Example:**
- Current cycle: June 1-30
- Upgrade June 20: 11 days remaining
- Charge: 11 × $0.63 = $6.93
- Next charge: July 1 (full $19 for July)

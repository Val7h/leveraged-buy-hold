## WEEK 1 FAILURES & HOW TO FIX THEM

**If blog not done by Jun 11:**
- Hire freelance writer (Upwork, $300 per blog)
- Use AI (Claude) to draft, human edit
- Delay publish 2–3 days (not ideal but acceptable)

**If YouTube videos not ready by Jun 14:**
- Publish YouTube scripts as written blog posts instead
- Publish video later (Jun 19+ is okay)
- Still hit launch date with blog + social

**If advisors not responding to cold outreach:**
- Shift to warm intros (ask existing beta users for introductions)
- Increase email frequency or try different angles
- Focus on Reddit/Discord instead (organic growth)

**If Discord/community not engaging:**
- Start discussions yourself (seed conversations)
- Host daily polls + AMA
- Post market updates + signals
- Don't worry if slow start (community builds over time)

**If CAC too high (>$150):**
- Double down on organic (blog + Reddit + YouTube)
- Pause paid ads (if any)
- Focus on referral program (Discord + community)
- Adjust messaging based on what's resonating

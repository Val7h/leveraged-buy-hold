TOP 5 ISSUES A RESOLVER:
================================================================================

CRÍTICO #1: BREAKPOINTS INCOERENTES (2h fix)
  Impacto: Mobile confuso em tablets (640-1024px)
  Causa: Faltam sm: e md: breakpoints
  Pattern: grid-cols-2 → grid-cols-2 sm:grid-cols-2 md:grid-cols-3 lg:grid-cols-4
  ROI: 10x

CRÍTICO #2: TOUCH TARGETS <44px (1h fix)
  Impacto: WCAG fail, difícil clicar
  Causa: Menu icon 20×20, botões sem padding
  Pattern: min-w-10 min-h-10 + padding
  ROI: 5x

ALTO #3: RECHARTS UNMEMOIZED (5h fix)
  Impacto: Performance lenta
  Causa: Re-renders + bundle 250KB
  Fix: Lazy load + memoize
  ROI: 8x

ALTO #4: TABELAS SEM OVERFLOW (2h fix)
  Impacto: Conteúdo truncado em <640px
  Causa: Sem overflow-x-auto
  Fix: overflow-x-auto em History + Watchlist
  ROI: 6x

ALTO #5: ZERO TESTING (25h fix)
  Impacto: Regressões invisíveis
  Causa: Playwright/Vitest não instalados
  Fix: E2E (10 tests) + Component (9 tests)
  ROI: 4x (long-term)

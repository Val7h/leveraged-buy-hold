## OBJECTION HANDLING

**"I'm worried about compliance."**
> "Totally valid. We're CVM-compliant and have legal review. Happy to share our documentation. One advisor asked the same thing and brought her lawyer to the call. All good. Want me to send the docs?"

**"This seems too good to be true."**
> "I get that a lot. The backtest is real (20 years), the advisors using it are real (50+), and the time savings are real. But the only way to believe it is to try it. That's why we offer the free trial with no lock-in."

**"We already use Interactive Brokers / eToro / etc."**
> "Totally fine. Most advisors use a broker. We don't replace your broker. We sit on top of it. You use our platform for leverage sizing + alerts. Clients execute in their broker. We integrate with most of them."

**"What if your system breaks? What's the backup?"**
> "Great question. You (the advisor) can always override manually. We don't lock you in. If system goes down, you still have access to all your data and can manage manually. But honestly, we have 99.9% uptime and are paranoid about reliability."

**"I don't have time right now."**
> "I totally get it. How about we schedule the demo for [DATE 3 WEEKS OUT]? That way you can plan for it. Or if next week works, even better."

**"My clients are too conservative for leverage."**
> "Makes sense. But here's the thing: Smart leverage (1.3–1.5x, Kelly-sized) is actually pretty defensive. Worse volatility than a leveraged ETF. And the returns are better. Maybe not for all your clients, but probably for your wealthier, longer-time-horizon ones? Worth an experiment with 2–3 clients?"

**"How much does it cost?"**
> "$19/month per client or $299/month for unlimited clients. It saves advisors $1,500+/month in time, so the ROI is obvious. And if it doesn't work, you cancel anytime."

**"I'd rather not share client data."**
> "Completely understand. You can test with just your own portfolio first. Then if you like it, you can invite clients. Some advisors do this and others don't. Up to you."

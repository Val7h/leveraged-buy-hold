## CRITICAL DECISIONS MADE

**✅ Activation Metric:**
- Primary: "User completes first backtest by Day 7"
- Secondary: Free → Pro conversion (15%), D7 retention (85%)
- Why: Backtest is most powerful feature; strongest engagement signal; directly tied to monetization

**✅ Onboarding Strategy:**
- 7-day email sequence (Day 0, 1, 3, 7)
- 5-step in-app tutorial (5 minutes)
- Escalating offers (free → $9.50 → $29)
- NPS survey on Day 7 (feedback loop)

**✅ Launch Date:**
- Beta: June 12 (internal + advisors)
- Public: June 19 (blog posts live, advisor outreach, full marketing)
- Gate: Must hit 200+ users, 85% D7 retention, <$100 CAC

**✅ Revenue Model:**
- Free tier: 5 assets, no backtest (80% of signups)
- Pro tier: $29/month → unlimited backtest + simulator (15% convert)
- Premium: $99/month → SMS alerts (2–3% convert)
- Enterprise: Custom pricing for advisors ($500–2k/month)

**✅ Go-To-Market:**
- Channel 1 (Organic): Content + SEO, $0–200 CAC, 60% of signups
- Channel 2 (Direct): Advisors + brokers, $0 CAC, 20% of signups
- Channel 3 (Community): Discord + referral, $50–100 CAC, 20% of signups

## RISKS & MITIGATIONS

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|------------|--------|-----------|
| Tutorial completion <50% | High | High | Simplify UX; test with users; iterate fast |
| Backtest performance slow | Medium | Medium | Monitor API response time; optimize queries |
| Email deliverability issues | Medium | Medium | Test with CVM; use multiple tools (Mailgun + Sendgrid) |
| Free → Pro conversion <10% | Medium | High | Lower price; improve offer; enhance positioning |
| D7 retention <70% | Medium | High | Investigate churn reason; fix friction points |
| Advisor channel doesn't work | Medium | Medium | Have fallback: affiliate program (30% commission) |
| Regulatory blocker (CVM) | Low | Critical | Contact CVM Day 1; have Plan B (SaaS only, no leverage) |

**Mitigation Plan:** Daily standups, weekly metrics review, fast iteration on underperforming areas.
